## Future master-artwork specification

For a new habitat scene, request a high-quality **master** rather than a screen-sized crop: target approximately 2048×1536 px (4:3 landscape) for composite plates, language-neutral with no embedded labels. At 1448×1086 current scene art scale, individual subjects can become small on a phone; visual review must set the final delivery density against actual target layouts before shipping. For the Underground scene, prepare same-canvas transparent RGBA layers for sky/woodland surface, soil cross-section, roots, tunnel/warren shapes and each independently selectable animal/foreground. A hybrid painted background plus clean transparent foreground layers is preferred if it keeps shading/edges consistent; vector is suitable only for simple soil boundaries/interaction markers, not as a substitute for detailed original animal art.

Create portrait and landscape compositions from the same approved layer art or verify that a responsive layout can reflow it without cropping key subjects. Touch targets should be authored/positioned to achieve at least 56dp actionable areas; where artwork subjects are smaller, expand invisible hit regions only if regions remain distinct and provide labeled control alternatives. Keep high contrast for selected outlines, neutral selection cues and accessibility descriptions.

## Provenance and reuse rights

The in-repository Animals README/manifests identify Wave 1 as generated/isolated and Wave 2/3 as project art packs. SceneDescriptions include generation prompts, metadata and visual-QA records. The asset directory does not provide a per-file SPDX/license/source-owner field in the manifests inspected. Before adding external art or redistributing a new asset, record source, license/permission, attribution obligation and exact asset version in its manifest. No external art was downloaded or introduced for this audit.
