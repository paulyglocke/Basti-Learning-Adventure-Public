Counts below mean authored semantic examples, distinct task definitions, or explicit objects. ## Count interpretation and current-state notes

- The 53 legacy verb records are one repeated data array supporting **both** Animal Actions and Learn Verbs; count once as content, list twice as entry/interaction format.
- The five native Maths activities deliberately use small reviewed ranges and repeat content in 5/10 rounds. Their possible answer-choice orderings do not represent more learning content.
- Tell Me’s 81 scenes and Prepositions’ 52 scenes are countable visual contexts; they offer more contextual breadth than the smaller sort/sequence starters.
- Legacy number and arithmetic activities have large numeric combinations but use repeated formats and emoji scenes. Their combination count should not be mistaken for equivalent depth or illustration variety.
- `app.js` still contains legacy implementations for Days & Seasons, Prepositions, and several modes; current Home dispatches Days & Seasons and Prepositions to native Compose. These retained implementations are compatibility code, not additional Home activities.
