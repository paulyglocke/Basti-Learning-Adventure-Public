# Freshwater artwork brief

**Requirement: new approved artwork.** Existing lake backgrounds are Seasons curriculum assets without target animals or underwater depth. The available Fish cutout depicts a clownfish. No Frog or Duck canonical standalone art is present.

## Deliverables

1. One language-neutral 4:3 pond/lakeside background, target master 2048×1536 px. Do not upscale a smaller raster; retain a crisp original-size master if the tool cannot produce this size.
2. Separate transparent PNG cutouts for a generic, non-species-assertive Frog and Duck, with whole-animal silhouettes, no text and enough transparent padding to permit clean selection outlines.
3. Editable source/master and asset record covering tool/workflow, prompt or illustrator brief, source material, modifications, export settings and reuse approval.

## Visual composition

Show a calm European temperate pond: sky and distant greenery, a distinct shallow grassy/reedy shore, lily pads or water plants, and visible water below the surface. Use readable shapes and negative space; do not embed the two selectable animals into the background. Position a frog at the water's edge and a duck floating on the freshwater surface. Keep animal sizes coherent and silhouettes legible on a phone. Avoid coral, tropical fish, ocean waves, text labels, dense reeds, tiny details and visually ambiguous sea/pond cues.

Match the friendly illustrated language and gentle lighting in the existing app art. Do not copy or modify the four Seasons lakeside files. Do not incorporate external image references into output unless rights and provenance are reviewed and explicitly approved.

## Acceptance

- The scene reads as pond/freshwater without labels.
- Shoreline, surface and submerged water areas are distinguishable and form one coherent scene.
- Frog and Duck cutouts are independently selectable and recognizable at phone size.
- Each separate PNG has clean alpha edges and consistent scale/style.
- The assets remain legible when a complete 4:3 image is fitted into portrait/landscape layouts; no important animal is cropped.
- The imported pack identifies semantic IDs only after approval; do not assign new canonical IDs by filename alone.
- Provenance and owner reuse approval are recorded; no unsupported rights statement is made.

## 5C.5B production record

The three requested images were created for the Freshwater implementation and
are present in the app asset pack: a 1448×1086 RGB pond background and separate
1312×1199 RGBA Frog and Duck cutouts with alpha. The pond is smaller than the
2048×1536 preferred master in this brief; it was kept at its generated native
resolution rather than artificially upscaled. It remains sharp in inspected
emulator viewports. All three are linked in the visual review. The cutouts were
edited to remove an initially baked checkerboard; the checkerboard versions
were not imported.

The creation record and limitations are in
[`../animals-phase5c5-freshwater-review/REVIEW.md`](../animals-phase5c5-freshwater-review/REVIEW.md).
No external image was incorporated. Generation details do not constitute an
owner grant of reuse or commercial rights; those remain to be confirmed before
release.
