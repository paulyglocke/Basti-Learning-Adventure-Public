## Generation brief

Create a polished, bright 2D cartoon classroom illustration for a young child’s educational language app.

Landscape composition, approximately 4:3. No text, captions, labels, logos or UI.

Educational purpose: FUNCTIONAL SCHOOL TASKS + ACTION VERBS + CLASSROOM NOUNS.

Use exactly five focal children:
- one child reading one open picture book
- one child writing on paper with one pencil
- one child cutting coloured paper with one pair of child-safe scissors
- one child gluing a paper shape with one glue stick
- one child putting one book away on a low shelf

The image must support:
- The child is reading a book.
- The child is writing.
- The child is cutting the paper.
- The child is gluing.
- The child is putting the book away.

Avoid extra children, teachers/adults, duplicated tools, hidden actions, anatomy errors, readable text, and visual clutter.
