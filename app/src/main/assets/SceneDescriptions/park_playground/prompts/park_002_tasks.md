## Generation brief

Create a polished, bright 2D cartoon park / playground illustration for a young child’s educational language app.

Landscape composition, approximately 4:3. No text, captions, labels, logos or UI.

Educational purpose: EVERYDAY TASKS, SOCIAL ACTIONS and FUNCTIONAL LANGUAGE.

Use exactly seven focal children:
- two children for GIVING one red ball
- two children in the sandbox SHARING one bucket and one spade
- one child PUTTING a toy into a storage box
- one child THROWING paper rubbish into a bin
- one child DRINKING water from a bottle

The image must support:
- The child is giving the ball.
- The children are sharing.
- The child is putting the toy away.
- The child is throwing the rubbish in the bin.
- The child is drinking water.

Avoid extra children, duplicate key props, hidden hands, ambiguous task actions, unexplained adults, or decorative clutter that competes with the five tasks.
