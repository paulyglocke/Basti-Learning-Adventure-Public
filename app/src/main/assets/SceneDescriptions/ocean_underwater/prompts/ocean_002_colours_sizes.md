## Generation brief

Create a polished, bright 2D cartoon underwater illustration for a young child’s educational language app.

Landscape composition, approximately 4:3. No text, captions, labels, logos or UI.

Educational purpose: visible properties, especially BIG/SMALL and COLOUR. Size differences must be deliberately obvious.

Use exactly:
- one very large green sea turtle
- one clearly small orange-and-yellow seahorse
- one medium-sized orange-and-white clownfish
- exactly three very small fish in a group, clearly different in colour from the clownfish
- colourful coral including pink/purple and orange/yellow
- a small number of clear bubbles

The image must support:
- The turtle is big.
- The seahorse is small.
- The clownfish is orange and white.
- The three fish are little.
- The coral is colourful.
- The turtle is bigger than the fish.
- There are three little fish.
- There is one turtle.

Strict size hierarchy:
LARGE TURTLE > MEDIUM CLOWNFISH > SMALL SEAHORSE / VERY SMALL FISH.

Avoid duplicates, extra background fish, weak colour contrast, ambiguous scale, hidden subjects, anatomy errors, or unexplained props.
