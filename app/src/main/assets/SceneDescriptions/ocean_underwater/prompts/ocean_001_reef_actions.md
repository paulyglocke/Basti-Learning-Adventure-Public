## Generation brief

Create a polished, bright 2D cartoon illustration for a young child’s educational language app.

The educational purpose is to make five different actions immediately understandable. Keep the scene attractive but uncluttered.

Use exactly:

- one green turtle swimming horizontally
- one red/orange crab carrying one seashell
- one orange octopus crawling/climbing across one large rock
- exactly three small fish swimming together in the same direction

Environment: clear blue tropical sea, sandy seabed, a few rocks, colourful coral, seaweed, bubbles and restrained distant reef detail.

The image must support:
- The dolphin is jumping out of the water.
- The turtle is swimming.
- The crab is carrying a shell.
- The octopus is crawling over a rock.
- The three little fish are swimming together.

Avoid duplicates, extra limbs, unexplained props, overlapping focal subjects, hidden actions and visual clutter.
