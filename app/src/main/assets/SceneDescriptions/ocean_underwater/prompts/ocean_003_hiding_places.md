## Generation brief

Create a polished, bright 2D cartoon underwater illustration for a young child’s educational language app.

Landscape composition, approximately 4:3. No text, captions, labels, logos or UI.

Educational purpose: spatial language. Every intended relationship must be visually unmistakable.

Use exactly:
- one red/orange crab UNDER a large arch-shaped rock
- one orange starfish ON the sandy seabed
- one green turtle ABOVE the starfish
- one yellow/orange seahorse NEXT TO tall green seaweed
- one orange octopus partly BEHIND a large pink coral formation
- exactly two small fish NEAR a separate coral formation

Keep all six examples spatially separated so an adult can point to one relationship at a time.

The image must support:
- The crab is under the rock.
- The starfish is on the sand.
- The turtle is above the starfish.
- The seahorse is next to the seaweed.
- The octopus is behind the coral.
- The fish are near the coral.

Avoid extra animals, duplicates, extra starfish or crabs, anatomy errors, ambiguous overlaps, human objects, unexplained props, and decorative clutter that changes the intended relationship.
