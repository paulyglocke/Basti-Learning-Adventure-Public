## Generation brief

Create a polished, bright 2D cartoon sky / flying-adventure illustration for a young child’s educational language app.

Landscape composition, approximately 4:3. No text, captions, labels, logos or UI.

Educational purpose: ADJECTIVES, COLOUR, SIZE, QUANTITY and SIMPLE COMPARISONS.

Use exactly:
- one very large red-and-yellow hot-air balloon
- one clearly small blue propeller airplane
- one long white cloud
- one small round white cloud
- exactly three small yellow birds
- one bright multi-coloured kite

The image must support:
- The balloon is big.
- The airplane is small.
- The balloon is bigger than the airplane.
- The airplane is blue.
- One cloud is long and one cloud is small.
- There are three little yellow birds.
- The kite is colourful.

Avoid extra birds, extra aircraft, duplicate focal objects, weak size contrast, ambiguous cloud shapes, anatomy/structure errors, and unexplained props.
