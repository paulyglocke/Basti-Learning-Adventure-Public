# Wash Hands step artwork

Four original images generated with the built-in image generation tool for the
existing four-step sequencing activity. Bundled copies are 512px JPEGs at
quality 88. Text and step numbers are rendered by the app, not embedded in art.

Files: `water_on.jpg`, `wet.jpg`, `soap.jpg`, `rinse.jpg`.

## Prompt set

Shared prompt: “Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: square picture card
for an offline children's sequencing exercise. Style: warm polished children's
educational illustration, softly textured gouache and colored pencil with clear
realistic shapes. Composition: close view of hands, a simple silver faucet and
pale blue basin against a warm cream paper background; small blue sleeve cuffs,
warm medium skin tone and a consistent four-card set. One clear action fills the
center with margins, readable at small size. Hands have natural anatomy, five
fingers per visible hand, no extra limbs. No faces or full people, no labels,
numbers, arrows, lettering, logos or watermark. Only depict this single step;
no panel grid.”

Distinct subjects:

- Water on: a child's hand gripping and turning a faucet handle; a clear stream
  starts. Hand beside the handle, away from the stream. No soap.
- Wet: two open hands together under a clear faucet stream; water touches the
  palms. No soap or foam.
- Soap: two hands rubbing together with visible soft white soap foam. Faucet
  off, no stream or soap bottle.
- Rinse: hands under a clear stream; small remaining soap bubbles wash into the
  basin. Mostly clean hands, visibly less foam than the soap picture.

These illustrations represent the activity's existing authored slice. They do
not add or reorder the learning steps or change checkpoint/progress identities.
